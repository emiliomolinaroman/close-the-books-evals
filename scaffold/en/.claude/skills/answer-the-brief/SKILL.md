---
name: answer-the-brief
description: For written deliverables graded with an accountant's rubric (reconciliations, accruals, variance analysis, proposed entries, "output in console"). Use it when writing and when reviewing each answer.
---
Learned from 20 real APEX-Accounting briefs graded with their accountants' rubric. Points were lost on how the answer was framed,
almost never on bad calculations:
1. ONE final figure per question. If you correct a workpaper, the corrected figure IS the answer; the workpaper's figure only appears as
   "the workpaper showed X". Two tables with different figures = the judge gives 0 even if one of them is right.
2. The framing the brief asks for, even if you disagree. "Output the reconciling variances": name every reconciling item with its
   amount, even if it is justified. Saying "variance 0.00, it is justified" loses those criteria.
3. No more and no less than what is asked. "Identify at most one entry" → one. Do not add entries, interest or adjustments nobody asked for:
   the rubric may have an "only one entry" or a "does not include interest" criterion.
4. Source: the files that come with the brief (memo, summary, draft) are the baseline. Recompute from the raw data
   only if the brief asks for it or the file has a demonstrable error; if you correct it, say which cell and why.
5. Break it down: every total with its components (by vendor, by bracket, by account, by area) and every amount that makes up a reconciliation.
   The rubric usually has one criterion per subtotal.
6. Look for errors in the prior work: miscopied rates, items subtracted twice, amounts in the wrong account, cents
   that do not add up (an entry of a few dollars can be a criterion). Cross-check every rate and every total against its original source.
7. Format: at the top, an "Answer" block with the requested figures, in the order of the questions, with the unit and rounding requested;
   below it, the explanation and the entries (account, debit/credit, amount). Before delivering, reread the brief sentence by sentence and mark which line
   answers each one.
