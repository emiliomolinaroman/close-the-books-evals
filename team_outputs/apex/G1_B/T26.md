## Method (code-computed, no intermediate rounding)
- **Net Realized Billing (NRB), §7.3**: approved billable time (billable = Y, approved = Y, Clio time entries) less invoice write-downs **and write-offs** per the Clio billing export, hourly matters only (contingency M-2024-0016/17/18 and the 9 flat-fee matters excluded). This basis ties to the GL: Clio write-downs $794,892.25 + write-offs $17,236.19 + WIP haircut $6,125.00 = 4900 $818,253.44, and to the firm's realization workpaper (hourly NRB $8,049,963.06). The task's billing_realization_summary uses modeled write-down rates, omits write-offs and its H1+H2 write-downs ≠ FY column, so it was not used.
- **Split origination, §7.4** (matter NRB > $250,000): Qualified Secondary Partner = other partner with most personal billable hours on the matter (≥100 h); 70/30, or 60/40 if QSP hours > Responsible Attorney hours.
- **Reassignment, §7.6** – M-2024-0020 Cascade Foods, Asante → Voss eff. Sept 1, 2024 (PA-2024-021): pre-Sept NRB (Jun–Aug time $182,617.50 − write-downs $23,111.28 = $159,506.22) to Asante, post NRB $171,319.55 to Voss; 10% Origination Recapture of the predecessor's pre-reassignment NRB ($15,950.62) moved to Voss. The $250,000 test uses the full-year matter NRB ($330,825.77) → split applies: on Asante's segment ($143,555.60) Voss is QSP (277.7 h vs Asante 39.0 h) → 60/40 (Asante $86,133.36 / Voss $57,422.24); on Voss's segment ($187,270.17) no other partner has ≥100 h (O'Brien 92.1 h, Asante 39.0 h) → 100% Voss.
- **Per-Matter Limitation**: max $400,000 credit per partner per matter.
- **Thresholds, §7.2/7.5**: Founding $600,000; Senior $500,000; Junior $350,000; Lookback Reduction (½ threshold) only if FY2024 AOC < 75% of FY2023 origination billing – not triggered for any partner. Diana Chen (equity admission July 1, 2024; no FY2023 history) gets the full, unprorated $350,000 threshold.
- **Marginal rates**: 10% on first $500,000 of excess, 15% on next $1,000,000, 20% above $1,500,000.
