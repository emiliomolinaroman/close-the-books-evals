## Method
1. **Pre-adjustment WIP:** the matter-level balances from the year-end WIP schedule (28 hourly matters only).
2. **Matter realization rate:** (Fees billed − write-downs − write-offs) ÷ fees billed, using each matter's own 2024 Clio invoices. This is the workpaper's definition. I applied each matter's rate to its pre-adjustment WIP (or to its capped basis).
   - **Workpaper error found – M-2024-0001 TechVista:** the realization workpaper shows a rate of 92.5938%. Its own figures give a different rate: net realized $277,146.72 ÷ gross $305,248.50 = **90.7938%**, and the Clio invoices agree. The workpaper rate is 1.8 percentage points too high (transposed digit). I used the corrected rate of 90.7938%. With the workpaper rate, TechVista would carry at $5,933.86 instead of $5,818.50, which overstates WIP by $115.35. All other 27 matter rates agree with Clio.
3. **Engagement modification – Greystone Ventures (M-2024-0021):**
   - The cumulative fee cap is $836,000.00 and cumulative gross fees billed are $834,400.00, so the residual capacity under the cap is $1,600.00.
   - The pre-adjustment WIP of $3,532.22 is above that cap, so the WIP basis becomes **$1,600.00**.
   - After applying the 98.0835% realization rate, the carrying value is **$1,569.34**, a decrease of $1,962.88. Cumulative gross fees billed are from the 2024 Clio invoices, the only billing history on file. Billings before 2024 under this 2022 engagement are not in the data; if any exist, they would use up the cap and push the Greystone basis to $0.00.
4. **Approved write-downs, applied after realization:**
   - **Allerton (M-2024-0007):** partial settlement with a 35% reduction. $18,577.18 × 88.9246% = $16,519.68; × 65% = **$10,737.79**. Decrease of $7,839.39.
   - **Mariposa (M-2024-0010):** partnership dissolved and fees released, so WIP is no longer collectible. Carrying value is **$0.00**. Decrease of $2,026.55, all of the WIP.
   - **Apex Robotics (M-2024-0022):** S-1 withdrawn; the client pays 50% and the other 50% is an approved write-down. $14,587.44 × 87.2648% = $12,729.71; × 50% = **$6,364.85**. Decrease of $8,222.59.
5. No rounding of intermediate figures. Final figures are rounded to cents. Flat-fee and contingency matters are outside the scope of this adjustment.
