   - Christmas is a *paid* holiday. Salaried associates earn their full salary for the 12-day stub (12/20–12/31), so the 11/14 factor is not appropriate.
   - The payroll accrual workpaper, the AJE description ("12/14 cycle") and the GL memo all say 12/14. The correct Associate wage accrual is **$26,373.63** (12/14 × biweekly salary), not $24,175.83.
2. **The booked Of-Counsel line is internally inconsistent.**
   - The memo says Of-Counsel uses 12/14, which gives **$15,164.83** (Mbeki $7,087.91 + Wagner $8,076.92). The booked figure is $17,362.63.
   - The $2,197.80 cut from Associates was plugged into Of-Counsel ("reallocation") so that the $64,631.88 wage total and the $69,814.33 entry still balanced. The "all balance checks pass" verification only proves the total, not the account split.
   - The correct booked entry is the workpaper's proposed JE.
3. **The AJE-03 allocation to practice groups was done wrong.**
   - Anthony used role totals × salary share and spread employer taxes and 401(k) the same way. Employer Social Security is capped at the $168,600 wage base, so it differs by employee (for example, Brooks, Park, Reyes, Mbeki and Wagner have $0 SS).
   - The correct allocation is the per-employee total accrual from the payroll accrual workpaper (wages + ER SS + Medicare + workers' comp + 401(k)).
   - Corrected AJE-03 by practice group: Litigation $33,251.11 (not $32,890.98), Corporate-M&A $19,508.94 (not $20,239.95), Real Estate $9,939.15 (not $9,568.27), Firm $7,115.13 (unchanged).
   - Examples of employee-level misstatements: Mbeki was allocated $8,473.08 against a correct $7,445.85, and Wagner $9,655.38 against a correct $8,484.81.
4. **AJE-2024-04 PTO: Hannah Whitfield (EMP-013, Real Estate) is understated.**

   - Her correct accrued PTO is 4.6 days × $519.23 = **$2,388.46**, not $2,379.30, an understatement of **$9.16**.
