## 2. Post-settlement holdback (administrator notice of Jan 9, 2025): new entry needed
- The competing claim was filed in **late November 2024**, so the condition existed at 12/31/2024. The notice arrived before the books closed. Under ASC 855 this is a **recognized subsequent event**, and the 2024 contingency fee revenue must be reduced.
- The administrator recalculates the fee on the adjusted gross. The cost reimbursement wire cannot be recalled, so the whole return obligation comes out of the fee:

| Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|
| Gross settlement | $1,800,000 |
| Less competing-claimant holdback returned to escrow | ($90,000) |
| Adjusted gross available for the fee calculation | $1,710,000 |
| Fee entitlement at 33% × $1,710,000 | $564,300 |
| Fee already received (wire A) | $594,000 |
| **Excess fee Sterling must return** | **$29,700** |

The other $60,300 of the $90,000 holdback falls on the client's net distribution. That is not a Sterling asset or liability.

**Proposed JE 1 – Dec 31, 2024 (reverse the excess contingency fee and record the return obligation)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |

## 3. Interest on the excess disbursement (Section 7.4 of the notice): new entry needed
- The $29,700 was not returned by the **January 10, 2025** grace deadline. Interest therefore runs **from December 15, 2024**, the original disbursement date, at **5% per year, accruing daily**, until the money is returned.
- Interest owed for 2024 (Dec 15 to Dec 31, 16 days): $29,700 × 5% × 16/365 = $65.0959, or **$65**.
- Daily accrual going forward is $29,700 × 5% / 365 = $4.0685, about **$4 per day**. Interest keeps accruing in 2025 until the funds reach escrow, and the 2025 portion should be booked in 2025.

**Proposed JE 2 – Dec 31, 2024 (accrue interest on the excess disbursement)**

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Interest Expense (non-operating; the chart of accounts has none, so add an "Interest Expense" other-expense account) | $65 | |
| 2050 Accrued Expenses (interest payable to settlement administrator) | | $65 |

## 4. Net effect and disclosure
- 2024 contingency fee revenue: $594,000 − $29,700 = **$564,300**
