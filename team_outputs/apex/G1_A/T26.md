## Method (Partnership Agreement Art. VII)
- **Net Realized Billing (NRB):** FY2024 net realized per matter from `billing_realization_summary_fy2024.csv` (gross approved time less write-downs/write-offs). Hourly matters only; contingency and flat-fee matters are excluded (§7.3).
- **Split origination (§7.4):** applies when a matter's NRB is over $250,000.
  - The Qualified Secondary Partner (QSP) is the partner other than the Responsible Attorney with the most billable hours on the matter, with at least 100 hours. Hours come from approved Clio time entries.
  - Split is 70/30, or 60/40 when the QSP has more hours than the Responsible Attorney.
  - With no QSP, the Responsible Attorney gets 100%.
- **Per-matter cap:** a partner's credit from any one matter is capped at $400,000 (§7.3).
- **Thresholds:** Founding $600,000, Senior $500,000, Junior $350,000.
  - Lookback Reduction (§7.4): the threshold is halved if FY2024 credit is below 75% of FY2023 origination billing. **No partner triggers it.**
  - Diana Chen, admitted 7/1/2024, has no FY2023 history and carries the **full, unprorated** $350,000 threshold (§7.5).
- **Marginal rates:** 10% on the first $500,000 of excess, 15% on the next $1,000,000, 20% above $1,500,000.
- **Reassignment (§7.6), M-2024-0020 Cascade Foods** (Asante to Voss, effective 9/1/2024):
  - FY NRB is $349,216.72, over $250,000, so the split applies (threshold tested on the full year).
  - Pre-9/1 NRB = pre-9/1 approved time of $182,617.50 × FY realization (349,216.72 / 378,760.00) = $168,373.34. Post-9/1 NRB = $180,843.39.
  - Origination Recapture: 10% × $168,373.34 = $16,837.33, moved to Voss.
  - Asante's predecessor share is $151,536.00. Voss (277.7 h) has more hours than Asante (39.0 h), so it splits 60/40: **Asante $90,921.60, Voss $60,614.40.**
  - Voss's successor share is $180,843.39 + $16,837.33 = $197,680.72. There is no QSP (O'Brien has 94.7 h, under 100), so Voss gets 100%.
  - **Voss total from M-0020: $258,295.12**
